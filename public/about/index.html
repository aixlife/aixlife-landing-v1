About AIXLIFE

Company profile · English

AIXLIFE (에익스라이프) is a software company in Seoul, South Korea, founded on January 26, 2023 and registered for computer programming services (Korean business registration no. 789-71-00438). We build AI agent ("AI staff") products that Korean small businesses and teams without developers can use, and we teach them how to build their own.

Products

  • AIMAX AI Staff — an AI staff service for small business owners: a web app plus a Mac/Windows local agent that drafts blog posts, quotes, and customer outreach and handles office tasks. Used by 3,000 users. Its writing and reasoning engine runs on the Claude API (Claude Sonnet). Developed by AIXLIFE; the service is operated by MakeFamily Inc.
  • AIxSCHOOL — launched in October 2026, a 12-month program and practice platform where non-developers build AI agents and chain them into automation teams (content, customer support, operations).
  • Custom AI automation — AI agents and business web apps built for client workflows, such as a consultation management app for a tax firm.
  • E-contract and client portals — an e-signature system and per-client training portals we build and operate ourselves.

How we build

Claude is our core model. We use the Claude API for product features and build with Claude Code. Stack: TypeScript/React, Node.js, Python, Vercel. Open-source work is public at github.com/aixlife, including a Claude Code plugin for non-developers (fire-your-ai-setup-coach) and council-cli.

Education track record

600+ hours of AI training and 5,000+ cumulative students for organizations including Kakao, the National Health Insurance Service, Hongik University, and Seoul Business Agency.
